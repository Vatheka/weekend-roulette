Two small models, useful jobs.

Recommendation search
TF-IDF turns descriptions and tags into numbers. Cosine similarity ranks relevant places, while your filters enforce budget, time, and suitability.

Satisfaction exercise
A Random Forest predicts generated satisfaction from pre-visit preferences, prices, and weather. It is separate from the recommendation ranking.

How did the model do?

Test attractions are held out entirely, so the same place never appears in both training and evaluation. Post-visit outcomes are excluded from inputs.
